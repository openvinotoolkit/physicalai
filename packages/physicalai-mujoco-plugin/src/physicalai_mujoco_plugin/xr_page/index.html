MuJoCo teleoperation

Connecting to the simulation…

  • You stand in the robot, life-size: its arms reach out ahead of you, the way you look at the start.
  • Hold a controller's grip button to move that arm: its gripper follows the controller. Release to stop; the arm holds. Grip again to continue from there. The trigger closes the gripper.
  • Or grab the arm where you want to move it: near the arm, dots show the gripper and joints you can grab; a ring marks the one in reach. Hold the gripper and the elbow with both controllers to place both.
  • A or X: bring the robot back in front of you. B or Y: show or hide the cameras.
  • Thumbsticks: left moves the robot down and up, right moves it nearer and farther (not while you hold an arm).
